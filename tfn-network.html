    Questions

    Where do the prices come from?

    The map shows the current price at each TFN truck stop, with the top 10 prices by region.

    Can I send the plan to my driver?

    Yes. Share the stops by WhatsApp or in the app.

    What is the fuel price outlook?

    A projection of next month’s diesel price, based on the Central Energy Fund’s daily data. It’s indicative until the official adjustment.
