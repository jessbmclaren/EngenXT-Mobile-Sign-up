Questions

Who provides the credit?

Standard Bank, Absa or Preference Capital. They handle the application and approval.

Who do I repay?

Your credit provider, not TFN. TFN supplies the fuel and the reports.

When do I repay?

Billing is usually monthly, with 30 days from the statement date. Your provider confirms your terms.

Can I limit what each driver spends?

Yes. Set daily, weekly or monthly limits per driver or truck, or approve each order.
