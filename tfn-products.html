Two apps, one hub

Today your drivers use TFN Pay and you use the TFN Dashboard. TruckersHub brings every product into one driver app and one fleet manager portal. It holds the things every product needs, so you set them up once.

The driver app

On the driver’s phone: see orders, ask for fuel, fill up and book a stop. Drivers only see what they need to do the job.

The fleet manager portal

In the office: set up your company, people, vehicles and rules, top up your wallet, approve orders and see every transaction.

Your company

Your company profile and documents, captured once for every product.

Your people

Give each person the access their job needs, from owner to driver.

Vehicles and drivers

Add them once and use them for fuel, cover, licensing and vehicle support.

Your wallet

Top up, see your balance and pay for every product from one place.

Notifications

Orders, approvals, low balances and anything unusual, as it happens.

Reports

Every transaction from every product, in one set of reports.

How TFN works

Open an account

Sign up online and add your trucks and drivers. Then choose cash (pre-loaded by EFT) or credit through your bank.

Order and fill up

Order fuel in the TFN Dashboard. Your driver fills up at a TFN truck stop, checked by location and photographed at the pump.

See every litre

Every fill lands in your dashboard straight away, ready for reports, statements and your accounting system.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us what you paid for diesel last time and how much you use. A TFN fuel specialist will show you what you’d save, within 48 hours.
