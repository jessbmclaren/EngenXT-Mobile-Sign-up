How Homebase works

TFN delivers to your yard

Order diesel for your own tank through the TFN Dashboard, on cash or credit.

Every fill is recorded

Each fill from your tank is logged against the truck, the driver and the litres, and only drivers you allow can draw fuel.

Yard and road, side by side

Tank readings, deliveries and on-road fills sit in one set of reports.

What you get

Know where the diesel went

Which truck, which driver, how many litres, every time.

Tank levels and deliveries

Readings and deliveries recorded, so losses show up as they happen.

Less reconciliation

Finance gets a clean digital record instead of chasing it afterwards.

Compare yard and road

See which routes use your own diesel and which use the road.

Credit across both

One credit facility covers deliveries to your tank, gantry collections and on-road fills.

A fee that fits

Your Homebase fee is set by your monthly on-road volume.
