    How a fill works

    Order

    Pick the truck, the litres and the stop. Or approve your driver’s request.

    Fill up

    TFN Pay checks the truck’s location and registration. A photo is taken at the pump.

    Done

    Litres, price, stop and photo are in your dashboard. Nothing to collect or capture.

    What you get

    Below the grid price

    Negotiated prices at every TFN truck stop. The more you fuel, the bigger the discount.

    Cash or credit

    Load your account by EFT. Or fuel on credit through Standard Bank, Absa or Preference Capital.

    Your limits

    Daily, weekly or monthly limits per driver and truck. Approve every order or let drivers order within them.

    Alerts

    Know about unusual volumes, new orders and low balances straight away.

    Accountant-ready

    VAT-compliant statements in PDF or Excel. Or send the data to Sage, Xero or your ERP.

    Subcontractors

    Give each one their own limits and your markup. Get one invoice.

    Questions

    Do my drivers need cash, a card or a code?

    No. You place the order or pre-approve the driver. The truck’s registration is matched at the truck stop. TFN Pay checks the location and records a pump photo.

    Where can my trucks fill up?

    At 270+ TFN truck stops across South Africa and on cross-border routes into Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe, Zambia and Mozambique.

    Can I pay without credit?

    Yes. Pre-load your TFN account by EFT. TFN is integrated with all major South African banks.

    Can the data go into my accounting system?

    Yes. Export statements to PDF or Excel, or connect TFN to Sage, Xero or your ERP.
