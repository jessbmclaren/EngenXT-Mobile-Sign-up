How it works

Set the rules

Add your vehicles and drivers, link each driver to a vehicle, and decide how much fuel each one can buy and how often.

The driver asks for fuel

In the app, before filling up. The request is checked against your rules and comes back approved, with a limit, or declined.

Pay at the till

The driver shows a QR code at the till. The money comes from your prepaid wallet, and the purchase is in your report that day.

Why it beats a fuel card

Nothing to clone or lose

There’s no card to skim or share. Each purchase needs a driver, a vehicle and an approved request.

Rules on every purchase

Tank size and how often a vehicle can fill up are checked before the pump starts, not after.

A wallet you control

Top up your prepaid wallet and see what’s left at a glance.

Drivers see only the answer

Drivers get approved or declined, never your rules or your balance.

Daily reports

See every purchase by driver and vehicle, the same day.

Set up yourself

Sign up, add your company, vehicles and drivers without waiting for anyone.
