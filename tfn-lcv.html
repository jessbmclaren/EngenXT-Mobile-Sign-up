How it works

Set the rules

Add vehicles and drivers. Decide how much fuel each can buy and how often.

Ask for fuel

Drivers ask in the app before they fill up. The answer is approved with a limit, or declined.

Pay with a QR code

Drivers show a QR code at the till. It’s paid from your prepaid wallet and in your report the same day.

Why it beats a fuel card

Nothing to skim

No card to clone, lose or share. Every purchase needs a driver, a vehicle and an approved request.

Checked first

Tank size and fill limits are checked before the pump starts. Not after.

Your wallet

Top up and see your balance at a glance.

Drivers only see the answer

Approved or declined. Never your rules or your balance.

Same-day reports

Every purchase by driver and vehicle, that day.

Set it up yourself

Add your company, vehicles and drivers. No waiting.

Questions

When can I sign up?

EngenXT is coming soon. Join the waiting list and we’ll tell you when it opens.

Where can drivers fill up?

At Engen service stations across South Africa.

Do my drivers need a fuel card?

No. Drivers ask for fuel in the app and pay with a QR code at the till.

Can drivers see my rules or my balance?

No. Drivers only see whether a request is approved (with the limit) or declined.
