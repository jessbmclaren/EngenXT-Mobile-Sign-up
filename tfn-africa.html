    Questions

    Which countries are covered?

    Namibia, Botswana, Zimbabwe, Zambia and Mozambique, alongside the South African network.

    Do I need foreign currency?

    No. You pay in rand from your South African account.

    Does credit work across the border?

    Yes. TFN credit works at cross-border truck stops.

    What else can I add for a cross-border trip?

    Tolls and parking, forwarding and clearing and cash for the trip, on the same account.
