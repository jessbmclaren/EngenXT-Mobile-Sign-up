# How Revolut and ElevenLabs change their brand per audience, and use colour to sell

Observed on the live sites (revolut.com UK pages, elevenlabs.io), October 2026.
Written to inform `BRAND.md`. Nothing here is copied; it is what we learned.

## Revolut: one system, five grounds

Every Revolut page uses the same typeface (Aeonik Pro, medium), the same nav
(Personal, Business, Company, Log in, Sign up), the same pill buttons and the
same section pattern. What changes is the **ground**, the **hero picture** and
the **voice**.

| Page | Ground | Hero | CTA | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal (home) | white page, full-bleed sky photo of a person in the hero | person plus a glass phone frame with a balance | dark pill "Download the app" | "Banking & Beyond" |
| Business | **black** from the first pixel | text only, no picture | **white** pill "Open a bank account" | "This is business banking", then stats (6%, 30k+, 4.9/5) |
| Cards | **black** | text first, product renders below | white pill "Join Revolut" | sections named by material: Premium collection, Make it Metal, The ultimate card |
| Kids and teens (6-15, for parents) | **white**, dark text | friendly, lots of space | dark pill "Open an account" | "Cards uniquely theirs", "Goals that pay off"; audience named in the small line above the title |
| 16-17 | light grey fade | phone and card on a ribbed dark block | dark pill | "Money freedom for 16 & 17s" |
| Plans (Metal, Ultra, compare) | **black** | the physical card in a darker material | white pill, text changes: "Join Metal", "Join Ultra" | price in the kicker ("Metal for £14.99 per month"), one persona line per tier ("for the smart spender") |

What this tells us:

- **Audience is signalled by ground, not by colour.** Everyday and younger
  audiences get white. Business, cards and paid tiers get black.
- **Premium means darker, not more colourful.** Metal and Ultra go darker and
  more material, never brighter.
- **The audience is named in the small line above the headline.** "Revolut for
  6-15s | Parents and guardians", "Cards", "Plans comparison".
- **Logo is white on black pages and black on white pages.** Never coloured.
- **CTAs keep one shape and change only their words.** Pill, always.
  Colour combinations are dark-on-white, white-on-black or black-on-white.
- A **sticky pill** at the bottom of the screen repeats the section's CTA
  ("Unlock subscriptions" on Metal).

## ElevenLabs: colour is identity and emphasis, never decoration on controls

- **The interface is near-white (`#fdfcfc`) and black.** The primary button is a
  black pill ("Sign up", "Choose Starter"). The secondary is a white pill with
  a soft edge ("Contact sales"). No coloured buttons anywhere.
- **Colour marks the product.** The switcher between ElevenCreative,
  ElevenAgents and ElevenAPI uses a tiny orb as each product's dot: warm
  orange for Creative, blue-green for Agents, grey for API. Each product's
  imagery follows: warm gradients for Creative, blue and green for Agents,
  monochrome grain and line art for API.
- **Upselling uses colour once.** On the pricing page every plan card is flat
  warm grey. Only the plan they want you to pick ("Creator", with a
  "Popular" tag) gets a full grainy gradient header. The eye goes there.
- **Every plan's CTA is identical.** Black pill; only the words change ("Build
  for free", "Choose Starter", "Contact us"). Business and Enterprise get no
  colour at all.
- **Promotion is a black pill and a struck-through price.** "Until Oct 18",
  "$6 First month 83% off".
- **The announcement bar carries colour for what is new.** A small coloured
  mark ("V4") beside a white "Discover" pill.
- **The assistant has its own colour:** a small orb button, bottom right.

## What to take for tfn

1. Audience by ground. Drivers white, Business black (already on the site),
   and a black plans page.
2. Product identity by orb dot. Fuel blue, Insurance violet and pink, Upkeep
   teal, shown as small orbs on a product switcher.
3. Upsell with one coloured card. In any bundle comparison everything is grey
   or black, and only the bundle we recommend gets the gradient and grain.
4. All CTAs are black pills (white on black). Only the words change per
   section and per bundle.
5. A sticky pill that changes its text with the section.
6. An announcement bar with the orb for anything new.
7. Premium is darker, never louder.
